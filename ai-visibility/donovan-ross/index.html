Executive Summary

Proven With Clients, Ready to Be Found

Overall AI Visibility Score

Donovan Ross has closed 18 homes in the last 12 months, from West Toledo to Delta and Helena, and every review a client has written about him is five stars. Search his name with "Toledo" and his RateMyAgent profile comes up first, so the reputation is already there. When a buyer asks a cold question like "who's the best Realtor in Toledo," his name doesn't come up yet, and the profiles that do mention him still list his earlier team at eXp Realty instead of Serenity Realty. The path forward is practical: put Serenity Realty on every profile, open a Google Business Profile so fresh reviews land where AI reads them, and publish a few pages that answer the questions Toledo and Maumee buyers already ask.

Three Strengths

  • Six reviews, all five stars, from buyers in Maumee, Oregon and Toledo, and three of them were first-time buyers.
  • Real production. 18 homes sold in the last 12 months, with recent closings in the 43615 ZIP, Delta and Helena.
  • A clear voice. His bio calls him a straight shooter who educates clients at every step, and his reviews say the same thing.

Three Opportunities

  • Bring every profile up to date. AI still connects him to his earlier team, and Serenity Realty doesn't appear next to his name yet.
  • Refresh the reviews. The newest ones are about four years old and live on one site.
  • Answer local questions. "Best Realtor for first-time buyers in Toledo" is answered today by directories and other brokerages' blogs.

Month One

Quick Wins

Authority Building

  • Claim Zillow, Realtor.com and Homes.com with the same bio

Content Consistency
