04 · How AI Picks

Understanding the Machine

How AI Decides Who to Recommend

When someone asks an AI app for the best loan officer in Westerville, it does two things at once.

1. What it already learned

The app was trained on huge parts of the internet, so it has an idea of who you are if enough places talk about you. Right now AI knows Chuck Holden is a loan officer with hundreds of strong reviews. It doesn't yet know you're the go-to person for first-time buyers on the northeast side.

2. What it finds right now

It searches the web, reads many pages, and pulls what they say about you into one answer. Then it looks for agreement. Do lots of websites say the same thing about you?

You don't win by writing one great page about yourself. You win when the whole internet says the same true thing about you.

The Three Engines

Everything in this playbook drives one of three engines, and if you skip one the machine stalls.

🔍
Be Found
AI searches the web to answer. If you're not in the results, you're not in the answer, so the older kind of Google work still matters.
✍️
Be Quotable
Write your pages in a clear, simple way AI likes to copy: short lines, real numbers, and true stories from your Westerville and Gahanna buyers.
💬
Be Talked About
How much the web talks about you, and whether it's good: reviews, listings, local news, partner pages. It's the strongest engine and the one almost nobody works on.

Layer 4 · Your Own Pages

Layer Four

Your Own Pages

All that attention is wasted if there's nowhere good to send the buyer. Your own pages are still some of the most-quoted sources in an AI answer. The goal is for AI to name you and link to your page.

Write the Way AI Likes to Quote

  • Answer first. Start each page with a clear, plain answer, then add detail.
  • Break it up. Short lines and clear headings, with no giant blocks of text.
  • Add a Q&A. Real first-time buyer questions with short answers, like "How much do I need to put down in Ohio?"
  • Use real numbers. Your review count, typical close times, and local facts AI can't make up.
  • Be a person. The Buckeyes, the Browns, Stella and Rocky. Buyers pick people, and AI repeats details that make you real.
  • Show your credentials. Photo, NMLS number, and years in the business, up top.

Layer 5 · Getting It Done

Layer Five

Getting It Done

The top reason this plan fails isn't the plan, it's the everyday hassle of doing it. Here are four friction points and how to beat them.

  • Time. You close loans, you don't run a marketing team. Keep it to a small routine you can repeat.
  • Approval. Some posts need a compliance check. Build approved templates once so you're not re-checking every post.
  • Company rules. Know what you can change yourself and what needs a request to your marketing team.
  • Sticking with it. Fix a page and AI may quote it within days, but Google reviews and outside mentions take months. The people who win don't quit in week three.
Your edge: 471 reviews means you already have the habit this plan needs. You're pointing it at Google, not starting from zero.
06 · Keep Score

Measure It

If You Can't See It, You Can't Win It

1. Ask Where They Came From

Add one question to your forms and your first calls: "Quick question, where did you first hear about me?" When someone says "I found you on ChatGPT," you know AI is sending you business, so do more of whatever got them there.

2. Do a Monthly AI Check

Once a month, open each AI app fresh and run your key questions. It takes 20 minutes and it's free.

  • Open ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI, and Claude in fresh windows.
  • Ask "Best loan officer in Westerville OH," "Best first-time buyer lender in Columbus," and your lane questions.
  • For each, write down whether you showed up, where, and what it said about you.
  • Note which websites the AI used. Those are your next targets for outside proof.
  • Track the trend month over month. The direction is what matters.

Toolkit B

Rules to Follow

AI visibility is marketing, and you work in a regulated field. This is general guidance, not legal advice. Check your plans with your compliance team.

AreaWhat to Keep in Mind
NMLS LicenseShow your NMLS number in ads where required. Keep your name, company, and license consistent everywhere. Only advertise in states where you're licensed.
RESPADon't pay, or get paid, just for referrals. Shared ads with agents or builders must be paid for fairly, for real work. Get it reviewed.

AccuracyKeep your numbers, limits, and program details up to date, including down payment help rules that change often.

One rule that covers most of this: Build your name on things that are true, clear, and said the same way everywhere. True keeps you legal, clear is what AI copies, and the same everywhere is what AI trusts.

10 · Glossary

Plain-English Reference

Words to Know

AI search apps
ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, and Google's AI. Tools that answer questions in plain words instead of just showing links.
AI Overview / AI Mode
The AI-written answer at the top of Google, above the normal search results.
SEO
Search engine optimization. The work of helping your pages show up high in normal Google results. It still matters as the starting point.
AI Visibility
How often, and how well, AI apps mention and recommend you when buyers ask about loan officers in your market.
Zero-Click Search
When someone reads the AI answer and stops without clicking any website. 8 in 10 AI-answered searches end this way.
Branded Search
When someone searches for you by name. They already know you exist. Your score here is strong.
Discovery Search
When a stranger searches for a type of help, like "best loan officer near me." This is where your gap is.
Sentiment
The overall feeling, good or bad, that the web shows about you, mostly from reviews. Yours is excellent.
Citation
A website AI used and points to as its source. Being cited means AI trusted your page enough to reference it.
Third-Party Site
Any website that isn't yours, like Zillow, a chamber page, or a local news site. AI trusts these more than your own pages.
Google Business Profile
Your free Google listing with your info, map pin, and reviews. One of the most important things for local AI search.
NMLS / Consumer Access
The national license system for loan officers. NMLS #1494178 is your official ID. Consumer Access is the public lookup AI uses to verify you.
Your Lane / Niche
The one main thing you want to be known for. Picking a lane gives AI a reason to name you over a generalist.
Answer-First
Writing a page so the clear answer comes first, then the details. AI prefers to quote pages written this way.
RESPA
Federal law banning payment for referrals. Key when working with agent partners. Shared marketing must reflect fair value.
TILA / Reg Z
Federal loan ad rules. If you list a specific rate or term in marketing, you may need to add disclosures.
