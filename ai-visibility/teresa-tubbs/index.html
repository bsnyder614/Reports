04 · How AI Picks

Understanding the Machine

How AI Decides Who to Recommend

When someone asks an AI app for the best loan officer in Troy, it doesn't guess. It does two things at once.

1. What it already learned

The app was trained on huge parts of the internet, so it has an idea of who you are if enough places talk about you. Right now AI knows Teresa Tubbs is a loanDepot loan officer in Troy. It doesn't yet know you're the person to call for a VA loan or a first home in Miami County.

2. What it finds right now

It searches the web, reads many pages, and pulls together what they say about you, good and bad, into one answer. Then it looks for agreement. Do lots of websites say the same thing about you?

You don't win by writing one great page about yourself. You win when the whole internet says the same true thing about you.

The Three Engines

Everything in this playbook drives one of three engines, and skipping one stalls the whole machine.

🔍
Be Found
AI searches the web to answer. If you're not in the results, you're not in the answer, so the older kind of Google work still matters.
✍️
Be Quotable
Write your pages in a clear, simple way AI likes to copy: short lines, real numbers, and true stories from Troy borrowers.
💬
Be Talked About

Layer 1 · Your Service

Layer One

Your Loans & Service

AI doesn't read your rate sheet. It reads your reviews, and so does every buyer who asks about you. Before AI talks about your name, it forms an opinion about the loans you do and what it's like to work with you.

What Helps

  • Get reviews often and keep them fresh. A few new ones each month beat a big pile of old ones.
  • Ask every borrower the same way, every time, after every close.
  • Send them where AI and buyers look: your own Google Business Profile first, then Zillow.
  • Ask for details, not just stars. "Teresa got us into our first home with a VA loan" tells AI your lane in the buyer's own words.
  • Reply to every review so AI sees you're real and active.
  • Make sure your loan types show up by name: VA, FHA, first-time buyer programs, new construction.
Compliance note: Never pay for reviews or give gifts in exchange for them. No fake reviews. Ask honestly and make it easy.
Layer 2 · Your Brand

Layer Two

Your Name & Brand

Ask AI about a loan officer and it'll tell the buyer what that person stands for. If you're known for nothing in particular, AI has nothing to say, so it says someone else's name.

Pick Your Lane

Your own bio points the way. Here are the strongest lane options for Teresa Tubbs in Troy:

  • Veterans and VA loans in Miami County. Troy sits a short drive from Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, and you already say veterans are who you love to help.
  • First-time buyers who didn't think they could. Your own words, and the most common question buyers ask AI.
  • New construction with local builders. Your Miami County Homebuilders membership gives this lane real proof.

Then Say It the Same Way Everywhere

Your loanDepot bio, your LinkedIn, your Google listing, and the way your Realtor partners describe you should all land on the same thing. Repeating it is the signal AI looks for, and drifting is what hurts you.

Quick test: Ask a past client, a Realtor partner, and an AI app "What is Teresa Tubbs known for?" If you'd get three different answers, this is the fix.

Layer 4 · Your Own Pages

Layer Four

Your Own Pages

All that attention goes to waste if there's nowhere good to send the buyer. Your own pages are still some of the most-quoted sources in an AI answer, and the goal is for AI to name you and link to your page.

Write the Way AI Likes to Quote

  • Answer first. Open each page with a clear, plain answer, then add detail.
  • Break it up. Short lines and clear headings, no giant blocks of text.
  • Add a Q&A. Real buyer questions with short answers, like "Can I buy with a VA loan and no down payment in Ohio?"
  • Use real numbers. Your typical close time and local facts about Troy, Piqua, and Tipp City.
  • Tell your own stories. "I've helped [number] veterans in Miami County buy their first home."
  • Show your credentials. Photo, NMLS 525388, and your 20-plus years where people can see them.

Layer 5 · Getting It Done

Layer Five

Getting It Done

Plans like this usually stall on the everyday hassle of doing them. Here are four friction points and how to get past them.

  • Time. You close loans, you don't run a marketing team. A small routine you'll keep beats one big push you won't.
  • Approval. Some posts need a compliance check, so build approved templates once and reuse them.
  • Company rules. Learn what you can change on your own and what needs a request to corporate (your bio page is usually the second kind).
  • Sticking with it. Fixing a page can show up in AI within days, but reviews and reputation take months. The people who win are still at it in week twelve.
Your edge: You've run a branch and your own company, so you already know how to build a system and keep it running.
06 · Keep Score

Measure It

If You Can't See It, You Can't Win It

1. Ask Where They Came From

Add one question to your forms and your calls: "Quick question, where did you first hear about me?" When someone says they found you on ChatGPT, you'll know AI is sending you business and you can do more of whatever got you there.

2. Do a Monthly AI Check

Once a month, open each AI app fresh and run your key questions. It takes about 20 minutes and costs nothing.

  • Open ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI, and Claude in fresh windows.
  • Ask "Best loan officer in Troy OH," "Best VA lender near Troy OH," and your other lane questions.
  • For each one, write down whether you showed up, where, and what it said.
  • Note which websites the AI used. Those are your next Layer 3 targets.
  • Track the trend month over month, because direction matters more than any single result.

08 · Rules to Follow

Toolkit B

Rules to Follow

AI visibility is marketing, and you work in a regulated field. This is general guidance, not legal advice, so check your plans with your compliance team.

AreaWhat to Keep in Mind
NMLS LicenseShow your NMLS number in ads where required. Keep your name, company, and license the same everywhere, and only advertise where you're licensed (Ohio, Tennessee, Florida, and DC per your bio).
RESPADon't pay, or get paid, just for referrals. Shared ads with agents or builders must be paid for fairly, for real work. This area is watched closely, so get it reviewed.
Loan Ads (TILA)If you list a specific rate or term, you may need extra disclosures. Skip rate claims that sound too good to be true.
Fair LendingAdd "Equal Housing" where it fits. Never target or exclude people based on protected traits.
ReviewsHonest only. Never buy, fake, or pay for reviews, and know that some states have extra rules.
AccuracyKeep your numbers, limits, and program details up to date, since old claims can create liability.
AI-Written ContentYou're responsible for anything posted under your name, even if AI wrote it. Check every number before it goes live.
One rule that covers most of this: Build your name on things that are true, clear, and said the same way everywhere. True keeps you legal, clear is what AI copies, and the same everywhere is what AI trusts.

10 · Glossary

Plain-English Reference

Words to Know

AI search apps
ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, and Google's AI. They answer questions in plain words instead of only showing links.
AI Overview / AI Mode
The AI-written answer at the top of Google, above the normal results.
SEO
Search engine optimization. The work of helping your pages rank in normal Google results. It's still the floor everything else stands on.
AI Visibility
How often, and how well, AI apps mention and recommend you when buyers ask about loan officers in your area.
Zero-Click Search
When someone reads the AI answer and stops without clicking a website. About 8 in 10 AI-answered searches end this way.
Branded Search
When someone searches for you by name. They already know you exist, and your score here is fair.
Discovery Search
When a stranger searches for a kind of help instead of a name, like "best loan officer near me." This is your biggest gap.
Sentiment
The overall feeling, good or bad, that the web shows about you, mostly from reviews.
Citation
A website AI used and points to as its source. Being cited means AI trusted your page.
Third-Party Site
Any website that isn't yours, like Zillow, a Facebook group, or a local news site. AI trusts these more than your own pages.
Google Business Profile
Your free Google listing with your info, map pin, and reviews. One of the most important pieces for local AI search.
NMLS / Consumer Access
The national license system for loan officers. NMLS 525388 is your official ID, and Consumer Access is the public lookup AI uses to verify you.
Your Lane / Niche
The one main thing you want to be known for. A clear lane gives AI a reason to name you over a generalist.
Answer-First
Writing a page so the clear answer comes first and the details follow. AI prefers to quote pages written this way.
RESPA
Federal law against paying for referrals. It matters most when you work with agent and builder partners.
TILA / Reg Z
Federal loan ad rules. If you list a specific rate or term in marketing, you may need to add disclosures.
