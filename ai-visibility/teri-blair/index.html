04 · How AI Picks

Understanding the Machine

How AI Decides Who to Recommend

When someone asks an AI app "best loan officer in Columbus," it doesn't guess. It does two things at once.

1. What it already learned

The app was trained on huge parts of the internet, so it already has an idea of who you are if enough places talk about you. Right now, AI knows a Teri Blair with NMLS #446179 works in Columbus. It doesn't yet know she's the loan officer who walks first-time buyers through the whole process.

2. What it finds right now

It searches the web, reads many pages, and pulls together what they say about you, good and bad, into one answer. Then it looks for one thing above all: agreement. Do lots of websites say the same thing about you?

One great page about yourself won't do it. You win when the whole internet says the same true thing about you.

The Three Engines

Everything in this playbook drives one of three engines. Skip one and the machine stalls.

🔍
Be Found
AI searches the web to answer. If you're not in the results, you're not in the answer. Regular Google work still matters as the starting point.
✍️
Be Quotable
Write your pages in a clear, simple way AI likes to copy: short lines, real numbers, and true stories from Columbus borrowers.
💬
Be Talked About
How much the web talks about you, and whether it's good: reviews, listings, local news, and partner pages. Almost no loan officer works on this, and it's the strongest engine.

Layer 1 · Your Service

Layer One

Your Loans & Service

AI doesn't read your rate sheet. It reads your reviews, and so does every buyer who asks about you. Before AI talks about your name, it forms an opinion about what you do and what it's like to work with you.

What Helps

  • Send reviews to your own Google profile, not only the office profile. Ask clients to use your name in the review.
  • Ask every borrower the same way, every time, after every close.
  • Spread them where AI and buyers look: Google, Zillow, and Experience.com.
  • Ask for details, not just stars. "Teri explained every step of our first home loan" beats a five-star with no text.
  • Reply to every review. AI sees that you're real and active.
  • Make sure your loan types and towns show up by name: first-time buyer, FHA, down payment help, Columbus, Polaris, Lewis Center.
Compliance note: Never pay for reviews or give gifts in exchange for them, and never post fake ones. Ask plainly and make it easy.
Layer 2 · Your Brand

Layer Two

Your Name & Brand

Ask AI about a loan officer and it'll tell the buyer what that person stands for. If you're known for nothing in particular, AI has nothing to say, so it says someone else's name.

Pick Your Lane

You can't be known for everything. Based on your 25 years and what your own bio says, here are the strongest lane options for Teri Blair in Columbus:

  • The First-Time Buyer Teacher: you already say you love educating buyers. Own it with plain-English guides, down payment help, and FHA know-how for first homes in Columbus.
  • The Broker Who Shops for You: your office is a brokerage serving all of Ohio. Explain in simple terms how you compare options for each buyer.
  • North Columbus Home Base: become the go-to name for buyers in Polaris, Lewis Center, Westerville, and southern Delaware County, close to your office near Polaris.

Then Say It the Same Way Everywhere

Your Lentor bio, your Google profile, your LinkedIn, and the way your Realtor partners describe you should all land on the same thing. Saying the same thing is the signal AI looks for.

Quick test: If you asked a past client, a Realtor partner, and an AI app "what is Teri Blair known for," would all three give the same answer? If not, this is the fix.

Layer 4 · Your Own Pages

Layer Four

Your Own Pages

All that attention is wasted if there's nowhere good to send the buyer. Your own pages are still some of the most-quoted sources in an AI answer. The goal is for AI to name you and link to your page, so you get the mention and the click.

Write the Way AI Likes to Quote

  • Answer first. Start each page with a clear, plain answer, then add detail.
  • Break it up. Short lines and clear headings, with no giant blocks of text.
  • Add a Q&A. Real buyer questions with short answers, like "How much do I need to put down on my first home in Columbus?"
  • Use real numbers. Local facts, typical close times, and Columbus market details that AI can't make up.
  • Tell your own stories. "I've helped Columbus families buy homes since 2001."
  • Show your credentials. Photo, NMLS #446179, and 25 years of experience, shown up top.

Layer 5 · Getting It Done

Layer Five

Getting It Done

The top reason this kind of plan fails is the everyday hassle of doing it. Here are four friction points and how to beat them.

  • Time. You close loans, you don't run a marketing team. A small routine you keep beats one big push you'll drop.
  • Approval. Some posts need a compliance check. Make approved templates once so you're not re-checking every post.
  • Company rules. Know what you can change yourself and what your office needs to change for you, like the old Motto pages.
  • Sticking with it. Some wins come fast, and a fixed page can show up in AI answers within days. Reviews and reputation take months, and the people who win keep going past week three.
Your edge: 25 years in this business means you know how to build a system and stick with it, which is exactly what this takes.
06 · Keep Score

Measure It

If You Can't See It, You Can't Win It

1. Ask Where They Came From

Add one question to your forms and your calls: "Quick question, where did you first hear about me?" When someone says "I found you on ChatGPT," you know AI is sending you business.

2. Do a Monthly AI Check

Once a month, open each AI app fresh and run your key questions. It takes about 20 minutes and it's free.

  • Open ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI, and Claude in fresh windows.
  • Ask "Best loan officer in Columbus OH," "Best mortgage broker Columbus OH," and your lane questions.
  • For each one, write down whether you showed up, where, and what it said.
  • Note which websites the AI used. Those are your next targets for Layer 3.
  • Track the trend month over month, because the direction is what matters.

Toolkit B

Rules to Follow

AI visibility is marketing, and you work in a regulated field. This is general guidance, not legal advice, so check your plans with your compliance team.

AreaWhat to Keep in Mind
NMLS LicenseShow your NMLS number in ads where required. Keep your name, company, and license the same everywhere, and only advertise where you're licensed.

One rule that covers most of this: build your name on things that are true, clear, and said the same way everywhere. True keeps you legal, clear is what AI copies, and the same everywhere is what AI trusts.

10 · Glossary

Plain-English Reference

Words to Know

AI search apps
ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, and Google's AI. Tools that answer questions in plain words instead of just showing links.
AI Overview / AI Mode
The AI-written answer at the top of Google, above the normal search results.
SEO
Search engine optimization. The work of helping your pages show up high in normal Google results. It's still the starting point.
AI Visibility
How often, and how well, AI apps mention and recommend you when buyers ask about loan officers in your market.
Zero-Click Search
When someone reads the AI answer and stops without clicking any website.
Branded Search
When someone searches for you by name. They already know you exist.
Discovery Search
When a stranger searches for a type of help, like "best loan officer near me." This is where your biggest gap is.
Name Collision
When another person with a similar name shows up in your results. Pairing your name with your NMLS number fixes it.
Sentiment
The overall feeling, good or bad, that the web shows about you, mostly from reviews.
Citation
A website AI used and points to as its source. Being cited means AI trusted your page enough to reference it.
Third-Party Site
Any website that isn't yours, like Zillow, Reddit, or a local news site. AI trusts these more than your own pages.
Google Business Profile
Your free Google listing with your info, map pin, and reviews. One of the most important things for local AI search.
NMLS / Consumer Access
The national license system for loan officers. NMLS #446179 is your official ID, and Consumer Access is the public lookup AI uses to verify you.
Your Lane / Niche
The one main thing you want to be known for. Picking a lane gives AI a reason to name you over a generalist.
Answer-First
Writing a page so the clear answer comes first, then the details. AI prefers to quote pages written this way.
RESPA
Federal law banning payment for referrals. Key when working with agent partners. Shared marketing must reflect fair value.
