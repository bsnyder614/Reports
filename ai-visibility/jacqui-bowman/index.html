Why This Matters

Homebuyers and sellers no longer start their search with a single Google query. Increasingly, they ask AI a direct question — and expect a confident, specific answer. AI can only recommend the Realtors it can clearly understand.

Questions today’s consumers ask AI
“Who’s the best Realtor in Stow, Ohio?”
“Best listing agent near Munroe Falls”
“Who should I use to sell my home in Cuyahoga Falls?”
“Realtor for first-time homebuyers in Summit County”
“Realtor who helps with relocation to Northeast Ohio”

AI builds its answers from what’s published, consistent, and confirmed across the web — website, Google Business Profile, reviews, local content and community mentions. When that’s thin, outdated or split across identities, AI fills the gap with a competitor whose story is easier to read.

How AI Picks Who to Recommend

When someone asks AI “best Realtor in [town],” it does two things at once, then looks for one thing above all.

What it already learned

Its training already has an impression of the Realtor — if enough places talk about them.

What it finds live

It runs background web searches and reads what the web currently says, good and bad.

Then it looks for one thing: consensus

Does the whole web say the same true thing about this person? That agreement is what AI repeats back to the consumer. You don’t win by writing one great page about yourself — you win when the internet says the same true thing about you, everywhere.

The three engines

Be Found

Basic search visibility — the price of admission. If you’re not in results, you’re not in the answer.

Be Quotable

Content written the way AI likes to copy: answer-first, short, real local numbers, first-hand stories.

Be Talked About

Off-site reputation — reviews, listings, local news, partners. The strongest engine, and the one almost no Realtor works.

The five sections that follow take these one at a time — where Jacqui stands, and exactly what to do.

Layer 1 of 5

Reviews

AI reads reviews, not resumes — recent, specific, aggregated reviews are what it trusts most.

Get reviews often, on the platforms AI and consumers check first: Google, Zillow, Realtor.com and Homes.com. Ask for specifics (“sold our home in 9 days, $12k over ask”), not just stars. Reply to every review. Ask within the first week after closing, while it’s fresh.

Layer 2 of 5

Personal Brand

One name, one lane, said identically everywhere — so AI isn’t left guessing.

Pick one lane you can own (first-time buyers, listings, relocation) and say it the same way in every bio. Test: if a client, a partner and an AI tool were each asked “what is this Realtor known for, and what’s their name?” — would all three give the same two answers?
