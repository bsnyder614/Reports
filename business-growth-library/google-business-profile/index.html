01 · Why Google Matters

The Foundation

Before They Call You, They Google You

Consumers search Google before they ever contact a lender. AI search tools rely heavily on the same information to decide who to recommend. A complete, well-built Google Business Profile is now doing two jobs at once.

An incomplete or missing profile does not just look unfinished. It leaves a gap that AI fills with silence, or worse, with someone else's name.

02 · Individual vs. Branch Profiles

A Key Distinction

Your Branch Has One. You Need Your Own.

A branch profile and an individual profile serve different purposes. Most loan officers only have access to the first one — and are missing the second entirely.

Branch Profile
Represents the Office
  • Builds visibility and reviews for the company location
  • Shared across every loan officer at that branch
  • Good for general company search visibility
  • Does not build authority around any one individual
Individual Profile
Represents You
  • Builds reviews and authority around your name specifically
  • Appears when someone searches for you directly
  • Gives AI a clear, individual record to reference
  • Yours to build, maintain, and grow over your career

03 · Building Your Profile

The Fields That Matter

Building Your Profile

Every field below should be filled out completely and accurately. An incomplete profile signals to both Google and AI that something is unfinished.

✓
Business Name
Use your full name exactly as licensed. Avoid keyword-stuffed variations.
✓
Primary Category
Select "Mortgage Lender" or the closest available category.
✓
Address
Use your branch address, or a service-area setup if you work remotely.
✓
Service Areas
List every city or county you actually serve — not just where you sit.
✓
Phone
Use a direct number you actually answer, not a general branch line.
✓
Website
Link to your personal page if you have one, or your branded lender profile.
✓
Business Hours
Keep them realistic and current. Update around holidays.
✓
Profile Verification
Complete Google's verification process fully — an unverified profile carries far less weight.
Best practice: Fill out every available field, even the optional ones. Google rewards completeness, and AI treats a fully completed profile as a stronger, more trustworthy source.
04 · Writing a Great Description

The WHO / WHERE / WHAT / WHY Framework

Writing a Great Business Description

Write naturally, for a real reader. Avoid keyword stuffing — Google and AI both favor descriptions that read like a person wrote them, not an algorithm.

W
WHO
Your name, title, and years of experience in the industry.
W
WHERE
The markets, cities, or counties you actually serve.
W
WHAT
The loan programs and services you offer.
W
WHY
What makes you different — your differentiator or approach.
Sample Description
"I'm a mortgage loan officer with over eight years of experience helping families across Central Ohio buy and refinance homes. I specialize in first-time buyer programs, FHA, VA, and conventional loans, and I pride myself on clear communication from application to closing. Whether you're buying your first home or your fifth, I'll walk you through every step in plain language."
Keep it natural. Include your markets, your programs, and your experience — but write it the way you would say it out loud to a client, not the way you would write it for a search engine.

06 · Reviews as Digital Trust

The Strongest Signal

Reviews Become Digital Trust

Reviews are the single strongest trust signal on your profile. They tell Google, AI, and future clients that real people had a good experience working with you.

Who to Ask
New Clients and Past Clients
Ask every new client right after closing, while the experience is fresh. Then go back through your past client list — those closings still count, and many past clients are happy to help if you simply ask.
Timing
Ask at the Moment of Excitement
The best moment is right after closing, when the client is celebrating. Waiting even a few days lowers the response rate significantly.
Responding
Reply to Every Review
Thank every reviewer by name and reference something specific about their transaction. Responding shows you are active and engaged — Google and AI both notice.
Sample Review Request

"Congratulations again on your new home! If you have two minutes, a Google review would mean a lot to me and helps other buyers know what to expect when they work with me. Here's the link: [your review link]."

08 · Google Posts

Stay Active

Google Posts

One useful post per week keeps your profile active in Google's eyes and gives potential clients a reason to keep engaging.

WeekPost Idea
Week 1Market update — a short, plain-language summary of current rates or local trends.
Week 2Neighborhood spotlight — highlight a community you serve and what makes it appealing to buyers.
Week 3Buying tip — one practical piece of advice for first-time or repeat buyers.
Week 4Community event or realtor interview — feature a local partnership or upcoming event.
Consistency over volume. A short, useful post published every week outperforms an occasional long one. Rotate through market updates, neighborhood spotlights, buying tips, community events, FAQs, builder updates, and realtor interviews.
09 · Questions & Answers

Answer Before They Ask

Questions & Answers

Answering common borrower questions directly on your profile helps real people — and gives AI clear, quotable language to pull from when it describes you.

How much down payment is required?
Down payment requirements vary by loan program — some options allow as little as 0% to 3.5% down. I can walk you through what fits your situation.
What credit score do I need?
Minimum credit score requirements depend on the loan program. I work with buyers across a range of credit profiles and can help you understand your options.
Do you offer VA loans?
Yes — I offer VA loans for eligible veterans and active-duty service members, often with no down payment required.
How long does pre-approval take?
Most pre-approvals can be completed within 24 to 48 hours once I have your basic financial information.
Why this matters twice. A real borrower gets a fast, useful answer. AI gets clear, factual language it can quote confidently when someone asks it a similar question.

12 · Your 30-Day Challenge

One Month to a Complete Profile

Your 30-Day Google Visibility Challenge

You do not need a marketing team to do this. You need one afternoon to start, and four short weeks to finish.

Week One
Claim or Create Your Profile
  • Search Google for your name to see if a profile already exists.
  • Claim it, or create a new individual profile if none exists.
  • Begin the verification process right away.
Week Two
Complete Every Field
  • Write your description using the WHO / WHERE / WHAT / WHY framework.
  • Add every service and loan program you offer.
  • Upload your headshot and at least four other photos.
Week Three
Request Five Reviews
  • Send your review link to five recent or past clients.
  • Reply to each review as it comes in.
  • Save your review link somewhere you can access it instantly.
Week Four
Publish Your First Post
  • Write and publish your first Google Post.
  • Answer two or three common borrower questions on your profile.
  • Set a recurring weekly reminder to keep posting.

Small improvements create lasting visibility.  Consistency compounds. The loan officers with the strongest profiles a year from now are the ones who started this week.
